The floor is the same every Thursday, and the dancing is not. People arrive from blues, swing, salsa, and from nowhere in particular, and the hour settles once the first song does.

## If you are new

Come in street shoes or dance shoes. You do not need a partner. Say hello near the door, dance the songs you want, and sit out the ones you do not.

We are at the Veteran's Memorial Club, 7–10pm. The welcome post on this blog has the rest of the picture.
