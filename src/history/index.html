History of dance

Partner dances share a long history. This map traces where they come from: rhythm and body from African dance, the couple embrace from European dance, and the styles — including blues and fusion — that grew from both.

A family tree of partner dance

Partner Dance Lineage

Most partner dances take their rhythm and body from African dance and their embrace from European dance. Latin dances add Iberian and often Indigenous roots, flamenco adds Roma tradition, and contact improv and 5Rhythms join through fusion. Select a dance to trace its lineage.
